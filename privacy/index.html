Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Gyanu Notes ("we," "us," or "our") provides free study notes and past exam papers for Class 8–12 students in Nepal at nawarajgupta.com.np. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Account information: when you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and class level. If you sign up using Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from that provider.
  • Usage information: pages you visit, notes and papers you view or download, and general technical information such as browser type and device, which helps us understand how the site is used and fix problems.
  • Messages you send us: if you contact us through the Contact page, we receive your name, email, and message content.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you download notes and past papers
  • To respond to messages and support requests
  • To improve the site and understand which content is most useful
  • To keep the site secure and prevent misuse

4. Third-party services we use

We rely on the following third-party services to run Gyanu Notes. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Firebase (Google) — for account creation, login, and storing basic profile information
  • Google Sign-In and Facebook Login — if you choose to sign in using these, we receive limited profile information as described above
  • Google Drive — notes and papers are stored and served from Google Drive
  • Web3Forms — processes messages submitted through our Contact page and delivers them to our email

5. Students under 18

Many of our users are students under the age of 18. We only collect the minimal information necessary to provide an account (name, email, class level) and do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information you did not consent to, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use basic cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with the third-party services listed above, to the extent necessary to operate the site.

8. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including relying on Firebase's security infrastructure for authentication and data storage. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information at any time by contacting us. You may also delete your account through your profile settings once available, or by request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at gyanuunotes@gmail.com or through our Contact page.
