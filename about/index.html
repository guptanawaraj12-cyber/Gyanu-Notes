Our story

Why we started Gyanu Notes

Gyanu Notes started with a simple idea: every student in Nepal should have access to quality study material, regardless of where they live or what they can afford. What began as a small collection of notes shared with local students has grown into a free resource covering Class 8–12, built to match the SEE and NEB curriculum exactly. We believe good notes shouldn't come with a price tag — so everything here stays free, always.
